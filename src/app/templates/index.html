🔍 Lost & Found AI Identifier

Upload a photograph of your lost item (even if you don't know the brand or have missing details). Our deep learning model will analyze the visual characteristics and scan the campus "Found" database to find exact physical matches.

How it works (Layman's terms):

Our Siamese Neural Network acts like a high-tech fingerprint scanner for objects. It doesn't just recognize that you uploaded a "laptop"; it learns the specific scratches, stickers, and wear-and-tear of your laptop. It converts your image into a mathematical vector (a list of numbers) and compares it against thousands of found items in milliseconds using a vector database (FAISS).
